# Arshidha - Module 06 Customers, Sites, Contracts, and AMC

## Goal

Build customer, site, contract, and AMC frontend screens.

## Pages

- Customer List
- Customer Detail
- Site List
- Site Detail
- Contract List
- Contract Detail
- Covered Assets
- SLA Terms
- Renewal Alerts
- Contract Performance

## User Roles

- Company Admin
- Operations Manager
- Facility Manager
- Finance Staff

## Required UX

- Customer and site data should be easy to scan.
- Contract details should focus on daily operational usefulness.
- Covered assets, service frequency, SLA, renewal, and billing relationship should be clear.

## Acceptance

- Customer detail connects sites, assets, contracts, service history, and finance references.
- AMC screens do not feel like a complex legal editor.
