# Arshidha - Module 10 Reports, Analytics, and AI

## Goal

Build focused reports and AI assistance patterns.

## Pages

- Management Dashboard
- Operations Reports
- SLA Reports
- Technician Performance
- Asset Downtime
- Maintenance Cost
- Inventory Usage
- Finance Summary
- AI Assistant
- AI Suggestions Panel

## User Roles

- Management
- Operations Manager
- Supervisor
- Finance Staff
- Company Admin

## Required UX

- Do not create 20-chart dashboards.
- Every KPI must answer a business question.
- AI should appear inside workflows as suggestions, summaries, and explanations.
- Natural-language reporting can remain a placeholder.

## Acceptance

- Reports are simple and useful.
- AI suggestion pattern works consistently across modules.
- Users can drill from summary to exception/action.
