## Important Rules

- Do not create a separate style for support modules.
- Do not over-design with too many cards.
- Do not add decorative animations.
- Use placeholder/sample data.
- Keep forms short and practical.
- AI should appear as a suggestion pattern, not as only a chatbot page.
