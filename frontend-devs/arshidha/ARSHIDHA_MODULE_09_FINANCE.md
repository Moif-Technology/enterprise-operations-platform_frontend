# Arshidha - Module 09 Quotations, Invoicing, and Payments

## Goal

Build simple service-business finance screens without turning the product into a full accounting ERP.

## Pages

- Quotation List
- Create Quotation
- Quotation Detail
- Invoice List
- Invoice Detail
- Payment Entry
- Outstanding Balances
- Customer Statement

## User Roles

- Finance Staff
- Business Owner
- Operations Manager

## Required UX

- Show customer, job, contract, amount, due date, and payment status clearly.
- Quote and invoice forms should be practical.
- Approval status should be visible where required.

## Acceptance

- User can understand quote, invoice, payment, and outstanding balance status.
- Finance screens connect to customers, contracts, and work orders.
